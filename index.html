Automation Crisis Mgmt

The Challenge: High volume of manual refund requests and theft reporting caused delays (high TAT) and operational panic.

The Action: Engineered an automated ticketing workflow linked directly to payment gateways and defined a strict "Process Logic" for theft verification.

Result: Reduced processing time by 70%, eliminated manual error, and restored customer trust during critical incidents.

Scaling Quality Across India

Scale Audits

The Challenge: Rapid geographic expansion led to inconsistent service quality and "silent failures" in remote cities.

The Action: Developed a Centralized Service Audit Framework and standardized SOPs, enforcing real-time reporting from local leads.
